The Artist

Meet Prem

Your special day, our beautiful mehndi art. Prem Mehndi Jaipur blends classic bridal patterns with the latest Arabic and royal bridal designs — creating mehndi that photographs beautifully, stains deep and lasts through every ritual, dance and toast. From full-hand bridal art to groom motifs and playful kids' designs, every hand gets the same care.

From the first enquiry to the final touch-up before the baraat, every detail is considered — design consultation, cone quality and application — so all you have to do is show off your hands.
